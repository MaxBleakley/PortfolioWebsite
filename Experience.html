Lancaster Hackathon

I had the honour in my first year of university to participate in a hackathon which was delivered in collaboration with several universities within the Lancaster area and GCHQ. This hackathon covered topics of AI and quantum encryption. Additionally, we were challenged with creating a facial recgonition system based on a dataset provided to us. This was done in python using a raspberry pi and CCTV camera.


Punk Security

Punk security delivered a capture the flag style hackathon in which teams competed in challenges with several different technolgy stacks in order to find flags. Within my team I was primarily responsible for identifying flags hidden within kubernetes clusters as well as docker containers.

AWS Workshop

In second year I have had the oppurtunity to participate in an ongoing series of workshops surrounding generative AI and cloud infrastructure. The first session which has been delivered was surrounding the history of Language Models and LLM's. We also looked at how these models can be deployed within the cloud, particularly looking at AWS's bedrock.
